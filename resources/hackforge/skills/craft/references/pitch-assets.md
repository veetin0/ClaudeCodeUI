# Playbook: Pitch & brand assets

- **Name and logo**: a short, sayable name; logo as hand-built SVG (simple geometric mark + wordmark), exported to PNG at 512/1024 px. Check it works small and in one colour.
- **Visual system**: 1 primary + 1 accent + neutrals, one or two typefaces (use the sponsor's brand kit from `challenge/assets/` when the entry is "for" them, if allowed). Apply the same system to UI, slides and video.
- **Screenshots**: Playwright at 2× device scale with seeded demo data — never lorem ipsum.
- **Charts**: real numbers from SCORES.md; label units and baselines.
- **One-pager** (PDF): problem, solution, proof, team, contact.
- **Slides**: one message per slide, big visuals (renders, screenshots, one chart), ≤ 15 words of text per slide where possible.
